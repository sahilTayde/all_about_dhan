# Exam note — Gliner, *Global Macro Trading*

**Cite:** Greg Gliner, *Global Macro Trading*, Wiley. **Chair:** phd_market.

## What the oral wants

Macro is **regimes** (rates, FX, geopolitics, holidays) and **positioning**, not a 1m MACD. The edge, if any, is knowing when **not** to express a view.

## Tokens for FTS

global macro, regime, NEWS_DAY, holiday, Ganesh Chaturthi, overlay, HOLD

## Desk mapping

- NSE holiday / BIG_NEWS → **HOLD** the customer ticket (`CUSTOMER_TALK`, EVENT_MEMORY).
- Do **not** retune MIX because a holiday replay looked green.
- Mixed indices / opening drive already in PERSONA_DESK — same family as macro **session** filters.
- India options: RBI / Budget / expiry week = analog memory, **out** of SCORE_SAMPLE.

Combine with Tulchinsky: macro is an **overlay** on a factory, not alpha #152. Combine with Derman Badly: a GDP story is a metaphor; premium may not pay it.

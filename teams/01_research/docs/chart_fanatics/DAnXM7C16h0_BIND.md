### Core edge (formalization · HYPOTHESIS)

Liquidity (resting stop orders) is the primary driver of bias + trade direction; entries are taken only after the intended liquidity is swept/taken, using strict opposing-sweep rules to avoid buying/selling into the wrong side of a trap.

### Observation protocol (HYPOTHESIS)

1) HTF context: identify the last meaningful swing extreme (High_H, Low_L) on the chart you’re trading.
2) Liquidity presence test (spoken: 'respect high/low then move away'): treat an extreme as 'active liquidity' only if price approaches it and then departs (i.e., shows it was engaged).
3) Impulse check: after an HTF impulse, verify whether opposing liquidity is present/formed. If the chart is 'one direction' with no clear opposing pool, do not force trades (patience / sit on hands).
4) Trap setup: if seeking longs in an overall bullish context where price first took highs, then only become eligible to attempt longs after the planned opposing low is taken (strict rule). For shorts, inverse: don’t short until the planned opposing high is taken.
5) Internal→external: on lower TF, use the local reaction structure around the internal liquidity sweep; but target the external pool (higher TF) that aligns with the swept liquidity.
6) Execution gating: take the entry after liquidity is taken (spoken: 'always targeting liquidity and taking an entry after liquidity is taken'). Avoid pre-sweep entries that get caught by continuation or re-building liquidity.

## Alpha factory vs one ticket

**SOURCE_FACT (Ch.1 + Ch.2 open).** Gliner describes **global macro** as a **strategy family** (discretionary, systematic, HFT, CTA/trend), not a single indicator ticket. Discretionary = top-down, many markets, long or short, directional **or** relative-value pairs. Systematic = model book, emotion removed, scalable, often **slower** in 2008-style stress than nimble discretionary (his claim in this excerpt). CTA = often automated trend, Turtle-style sizing mentioned as the book’s later sizing tradition. Process chapter: **first** question is how much you can lose; log thesis/conviction/outcome; evolve the process.

He cites **other shops’** factory scale (systematic giants, CTA names) as industry context. That is **not** a WorldQuant expression factory and **not** our MIX catalog.

**Desk map (HYPOTHESIS).** Macro is an **overlay / HOLD**, not alpha #152 and not a second live book. One customer ticket stays. We do not allocate like a pension to “macro vs equity L/S.” KEEP_ALL STRATs stay in the drawer; they are not four simultaneous live macro books.

## Research status (updated 2026-11-12, E31)

**Objective.** Find a rule with a reproducible edge after realistic costs, or prove there is none in the data we have.
"Better" means the system can tell real opportunities from noise and stays out when it has no edge. Trading more is not the goal.

**Live strategies.** None. No live orders. Paper and shadow only.

**Forward shadow (running daily from the recorder tape).** These rows are not live results.
- FIN1: buy the straddle on active openings.
- S2: sell the straddle on calm openings.
- Adoption rule: 40+ logged days and p < 0.05 outside Oct-2026. Status: 3 days logged (16/21/22 Oct). FIN1 net -7.5k over 4 signals; S2 net -3.9k over 2.

**2026-11-12 round E23-E26 (casino-edge round; frozen with Fable review, `scripts/lab5y/exec_lab.py`, `casino_lab.py`).** Execution is not an edge: resting orders lose to adverse selection, and the engine's p75 spread is about the measured full-size crossing cost. **FIN1's +1.19L / +1.19L is withdrawn** (D7). No book passes. Then E27-E30 (Fable's four candidates from a review of the whole catalogue: in-window fade credit spread, delta-hedged short straddle on the vol forecast, B1-ITM, expiry-day iron fly) were all killed on dev, and a deterministic paper DESK now decides trade / no-trade / size each day (all strategies log-only). B1 is the only stream with a positive shrunk expectation (small, p_boot 0.25 dev). The S-family is one bet. Deviations from the frozen E23-E26 spec are listed under D7.

**2026-11-12 E31 (founder request: ~1 lakh variants).** 247,118 variants of 25 families (every family in this file plus ORB/VWAP/gap/EMA/MACD/supertrend/RSI/inside-bar/OI-flow/time-of-day/strangle/condor grids) x scenario gates, priced on `sell_lab.struct`, selected by walk-forward + Hansen SPA + placebo (rules frozen first). GLOBAL SPA p 0.93: no variant beats luck; all 25 families PARKED. See the E31 row and the results section below.

**Most promising candidates.** None proven. Experiments done: R0-R3, R3b, E1-E9, E12-E22 (E15 blocked: no data), B1-B9 (classic book rules, all rejected); audits A1 and A2 (both closed 2026-10-28). Since A2, every engine flattens at 15:15, the same as the FIN1/S2 shadow. Ranked by remaining hope:
1. B1 (shadow), and SGATE / SC1 (forward-only, one bet with S2). FIN1 is undecidable on history (D7); the wider forward tape can decide it.
2. The fade credit spread (E5b), undecidable on the history and needs the wider tape.
3. Overnight short straddle (E19): undecidable on the history. A model price that is generous to the seller puts it at about +5L in both samples (dev p 0.29). The forward tape can decide it, but only if the recorder snapshots the HELD strike at 09:20: a large share of nights gap more than 5 steps.

**Important discoveries.**
- **D1.** Dhan expired-options history is ATM+-3 only. ATM+-4..10 was refused on 728/728 calls.
  - On trend days the held strike vanishes, so anything held to 15:15 and any wing structure is undecidable on history.
  - The round-2 buyer engine exited early there, which biased rounds 2-3 for 15:15 holds.
- **D2.** How much the rest of the day moves (RV vs IV) IS predictable at 09:45.
  - Opening RV/IV gives AUC 0.74 on dev and 0.86 on holdout.
  - A walk-forward logistic model gives 0.70 / 0.87.
  - But option P&L is not predictable: S1 win AUC was 0.445 on the holdout.
  - Prices already include the predictable part of volatility. This explains why every timing rule failed out of sample.
- **D3.** After 09:45, breakouts failed more than random in calm Dec-Jul (dev p_vs_rand 0.91-0.99). That did not hold in Aug-Oct.
  - Pattern behaviour is regime-dependent.
  - The regime-to-P&L link does not survive out of sample (D2).
- **D4.** A random intraday ATM buy loses Rs 1.0-1.3k per trade (10 lots) to spread, costs and theta. Any buyer signal must clear that.

- **D5.** Until audit A2 every backtest flattened at the **15:00** open (FLAT = minute 900), while the FIN1/S2 shadow exits at 15:15. Founder decision (2026-10-28): one rule, **15:15 everywhere** (FLAT = 915), matching the shadow. Rows up to E20 were computed at 15:00. A2 re-ran the headline rows at 15:15 with no verdict change; E19-E20 were not re-run (E19 is overnight and does not use FLAT).
- **D6.** The SENSEX index print lags NIFTY by a few seconds: the NIFTY 1m return predicts the next SENSEX 1m return with IC 0.10-0.16 in every year 2021-2026. It also lags SENSEX's own options (IC 0.175). SENSEX option prices already carry the move by the next open (open->close IC 0.004, t 0.95), so it cannot be traded on 1m bars.

- **D7 (2026-11-12).** `opt1y_lab.trade` exits a leg at its last close at the FIRST missing bar. Gaps cluster where the strike leaves ATM+-3 at the peak of a big move, so for long holds this is an exit the rule never specified, and it flatters both long and short legs. FIN1 (held to 15:15) is +1.19L / +1.19L under it but -11.6L / -5.9L (cons) .. +8.3L / +4.8L (opt) under `sell_lab.struct` bounds: undecidable. Rows built on `opt1y_lab.trade` with long holds (R2-F, R3, R3b, FIN1) carry the same caveat. Rows on `sell_lab.struct` (E2+, B1-B10, SC, E21) are unaffected: B1 / S-family trades touch a bound in 0-1.6% of cases and every sign survives the pessimistic bound.
  Disclosed deviations from the E23-E26 freeze: (1) the frozen E23 ratio (per-leg floor, vs the decision mid) let 1-s price drift into cost; the engine check used a drift-free measure (quoted half-spread + depth walk vs the engine table), unfloored ratios reported; (2) 8 estimation days (6 at 09:45), not about 14; (3) FIN1 re-run on `struct`; earlier FIN1 claims retracted; (4) SGATE was defined after its parts were known, so its holdout p is not out of sample; (5) E24 correlations and ruin use the whole year incl. the used holdout; (6) the fresh test shrank to 1 day (10-28 NO_DATA) with shadow fills, and SC1/SGATE/B1 could not be evaluated; (7) the E25 FIN1 formal pass is voided by judgment (it beats a bound-priced loss); (8) the policy grid was cut to M30 at the freeze (Fable), before any result.
